# AI Safety Communications Course

A course specification for a **25-hour, five-unit course in AI safety communications** —
for people entering AI safety comms, media, advocacy and public engagement rather than
technical research.

> Mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority alongside other
> societal-scale risks such as pandemics and nuclear war.
>
> — Center for AI Safety, 30 May 2023. Twenty-two words.

The most effective piece of communication this field has produced was one sentence with no
mechanism, no timeline and no policy ask. This course is about why that worked, why the open
letter published eight weeks earlier did not, and how to build the next one.

## The shape

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 25 hours |
| Structure | 5 units, 3h prep + 2h live each |
| Pace | 5 days or 5 weeks |
| Cohort | 8 people |
| Cost | Free |
| Prerequisite | BlueDot AGI Strategy, or equivalent |

One artifact runs through all five units and is never finished until it is public. The
revision is the graded object, not the draft.
